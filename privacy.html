EtAI Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Who this applies to

EtAI is a private assistant used only by its owner, Dr. Etai Yaffe. The only Google accounts it accesses are his own, which he connects himself through Google's sign-in.

What data EtAI accesses

With the owner's permission, EtAI can read and manage Gmail messages, Google Calendar events, Google Drive files, Google Docs, Google Sheets and Google Contacts in the owner's connected accounts, only to carry out tasks he asks for.

How data is stored

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. EtAI does not run any server that collects or stores Google user data.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, not shared with third parties for advertising, and not used for anything other than the owner's own requests. To answer a request, relevant content may be processed by the AI model provider the owner has chosen.

Limited Use

EtAI's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and stored tokens can be deleted from the owner's computer.
